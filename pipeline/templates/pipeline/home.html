How It Works

Four simple steps from raw data to actionable insights.

1

Upload

Drag & drop .fq.gz / .fastq.gz reads, select a reference genome, and map each sample to biological conditions, batches, and timepoints.

2

Process

FastQC quality check, Trimmomatic adapter trimming, HISAT2 alignment, featureCounts quantification, batch correction, and DESeq2 differential expression.

3

Explore

Interactive PCA, UMAP, Volcano & MA plots, DEG heatmaps. Download CRAM files, raw & normalized count matrices, DEG tables, and MultiQC reports.

4

Advance

Deconvolve bulk RNA into pseudo-single-cell profiles, then unlock trajectory inference, spatial tissue mapping, and Moran’s I autocorrelation.

Platform Capabilities

From raw reads to spatial maps, every analysis module at your fingertips.

Alignment & QC

FastQC, Trimmomatic, HISAT2 alignment, featureCounts quantification, and compressed CRAM output.

DEG & Statistics

Batch correction (ComBat-seq), DESeq2 differential expression, PCA, UMAP, and Volcano plots.

Cell Deconvolution

DestVI / BayesPrism unmixes bulk RNA into pseudo-single-cell profiles for advanced downstream analysis.

Spatial Mapping

Tangram deep learning projects imputed cells onto H&E tissue slides with Moran’s I autocorrelation.
