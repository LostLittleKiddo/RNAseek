PCA Plot

Principal Component Analysis reduces high-dimensional gene expression data into two axes that capture the largest variance. Samples that cluster together share similar expression profiles.

UMAP Plot

UMAP preserves local neighborhood structure and highlights clusters of similar samples more sharply than PCA, especially in datasets with complex structure.

Volcano Plot

Each dot is a gene. Genes in the upper-left (blue, downregulated) and upper-right (red, upregulated) corners are the most biologically interesting. Dashed lines mark your chosen thresholds.

MA Plot

Shows the relationship between average expression level (x-axis) and fold change (y-axis). Red points are statistically significant DEGs. A balanced cloud centered on y=0 indicates good normalization.
