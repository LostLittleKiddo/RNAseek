 Cell Deconvolution & Imputation

Mathematically unmix your bulk RNA data into individual cell types. Generates a pseudo-single-cell .h5ad matrix that unlocks Trajectory, Spatial, and Autocorrelation modules.

 Trajectory Inference

PAGA / Pseudotime mapping of developmental lineages. Select a root cell type to compute differentiation trajectories from the imputed single-cell matrix.

 Spatial Mapping

Tangram deep learning maps imputed cells onto H&E tissue slides, producing spatially resolved gene expression maps from your deconvolved data.

 Spatial Autocorrelation

Moran’s I analysis identifies spatially clustered gene expression patterns, revealing genes whose expression is significantly correlated with tissue architecture.
